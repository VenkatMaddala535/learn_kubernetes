Get info
    use -n to get info of particular Namespaces
    use -A to get all running and dead processes
     kubectl get all -A
     kubectl get all
     kubectl get pod/pods
     kubectl get pods -o wide
     kubectl get deployment/deployments
     kubectl get replicaset/replicasets
     kubectl get svc/service/services
     kubectl get namespace/namespaces
     kubectl get statefulset/statefulsets
     kubectl get job/jobs
     kubectl get cronjob/cronjob
     kubectl get daemonset/daemonsets
     kubectl get nodes
     kubectl get all -n <namespace>

Describe
    kubectl describe pod/replicasets/deployments <NAME OF POD/replicaset/service/deployment">

Delete
    kubectl delete pod <POD Name> (if its created by a pod then it delete permentely or if by deployment it deleted and  instantly new pod created)

Create Namespaces   
    kubectl create namespace <Namespace>
